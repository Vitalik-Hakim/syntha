# S1 — executable calibration surface (PRD v3 §5.1)

**Verdict on the discovery set D (2025-07 … 2025-12, 2026-02 … 2026-05): no candidate.**
Registration: `registration.yaml` (committed 2026-09-15 before the first census month).
Report: `report/report.md` (generated by `tools/research/s1_calibration.py discovery`), with
`calibration.parquet` (every primary cell), `calibration_ultra_short.parquet` (the excluded
series, measured), `s2_contrasts.parquet`, heatmaps per offset and `summary.json`.

## What was measured

1,851,402 token-observations from ten census months (146,000 markets sampled from frames of
8,730–280,000 per month), one row per (market, token, offset) at −7d, −3d, −24h, −6h, −1h,
−15m before the scheduled end. Per primary cell (11 categories × 10 fill-price buckets × 6
offsets = 660 register rows): the executable return r = (payout − fill − fee) / fill under
CURRENT fees, cluster-bootstrapped by event and by UTC day (10,000 resamples, wider interval
shown), fill rate, mid calibration, capacity and MDE@80%; then the pre-registered rule with BH
at 10% FDR across the 660 cells.

## What happened, in order

1. First pass selected one cell (crypto [0.35,0.50) at −15m, +28%, all ten months positive).
   Diagnostics showed it was the 5/15-minute "Up or Down" series, listed days ahead and so
   misclassified as multi-day, plus a fill-model artefact: the 10-minute VWAP of prints at
   ≤ mid + 1 tick averages a fast market's dips (both tokens of 3,378 markets "filled" at 0.44).
2. Second pass (window parser) still let the 2025 *hourly* series through; three crypto cells.
3. Third pass excludes both (29,319 markets): **no cell passes BH**. Details in
   docs/DATA_NOTES.md, "M9 discovery".

## The negative space

Only three cells reach the registration's n ≥ 500, nine reach 300, all in sports; every one's
interval spans zero. The census could not have detected an edge smaller than 8–22¢ per dollar
in those cells and nothing at all in the rest: outside the last hour, fewer than 3% of
observations had an executable fill. A calibration edge in ordinary markets, if it exists, is
smaller than this census can see — or lives in the last hour of ultra-short markets, where the
measurement is a latency contest the programme does not enter.

## Confirmation

No candidate reached C1/C2. The confirmation tooling (`tools/research/s1_candidates.py`,
`tools/research/confirm.py`) is in place for later months.
